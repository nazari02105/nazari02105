## About me

I am a first-year M.Sc. student in Computer Science at **Dresden University of Technology (TU Dresden)**. I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from **Sharif University of Technology**.

My research is about building and evaluating **reliable and efficient AI systems**. I have co-authored five papers, at venues including **ICML**, **ACL Findings** and **CVPR**. I have worked with research groups in Germany, Denmark and the United States.

I have also worked in industry as an **AI Engineer**, building agentic LLM systems with LangGraph and LangChain for legal contracts, engineering documents and financial services. Earlier, I worked on software and data engineering: C#/ASP.NET back ends, ETL pipelines, web apps and networked systems. Many of these projects are on this profile.

## 🧪 Research experience

**Reasoning and safety of large reasoning models** · *University of California, Riverside, USA* · May 2025 – May 2026<br>
Supervisor: Prof. Nael Abu-Ghazaleh<br>
Modeled the chain-of-thought of large reasoning models as a six-state finite state machine, to find which state transitions lead to correct or wrong answers. Helped develop a training-free, Q-value-guided activation-steering method that improves reasoning on math and science benchmarks with fewer interventions.

**CLIP in multi-object scenes** · *Sharif University of Technology, Iran* · Feb 2024 – Apr 2025<br>
Supervisors: Prof. Mohammad Hossein Rohban and Prof. Mahdieh Soleymani Baghshah<br>
Studied CLIP's biases toward objects mentioned first in a caption and toward larger objects in an image, and their effect on retrieval, classification, image-text matching and text-to-image generation. Helped build the ComCO dataset and led all text-encoder experiments.

**Image retrieval for medical imaging** · *Sharif University of Technology, Iran* · Nov 2023 – Feb 2024<br>
Supervisor: Prof. Mohammad Hossein Rohban<br>
Improved histology image retrieval with a semi-supervised approach and a new re-ranking method that combines bi-encoder and cross-encoder models. Found a critical validation bug while comparing DINO with a supervised baseline.

**Protein language model** · *Helmholtz Centre for Infection Research, Germany* · Jul 2023 – Nov 2023<br>
Supervisor: Prof. Alice Carolyn McHardy<br>
Built a protein language model to predict antibiotic resistance across bacterial species. Used custom BPE tokenization and distributed training with FlashAttention and DeepSpeed, and explored morphology-aware tokenization.

**Super-resolution for 3D dental imaging** · *Aarhus University, Denmark* · Mar 2023 – Jul 2023<br>
Supervisor: Dr. Hossein Mohammad-Rahimi<br>
Developed deep learning super-resolution models for dental cone-beam CT images. Simulated image degradation with downscaling, blurring and noise.
