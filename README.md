## About Me

I am an M.Sc. Computer Science student at **Dresden University of Technology (TU Dresden)**, with a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from **Sharif University of Technology**. My research interests focus on developing and evaluating reliable, efficient AI systems, particularly in **large language models (LLMs), natural language processing (NLP), vision-language models, and representation learning**.

My research experience includes reasoning-trajectory analysis and inference-time steering for large reasoning models, morphology-aware tokenization, analysis of CLIP in multi-object settings, medical-image retrieval, and deep-learning-based medical-image enhancement. I have co-authored papers at **ICML 2026, Findings of ACL 2026, CVPR 2025**, the **ECCV 2024 EVAL-FoMo Workshop**, and in *Imaging Science in Dentistry*.

I also have experience in teaching computer science and building software and data-engineering applications. I enjoy work that combines careful experimentation, model evaluation, and practical implementation.

## Research Interests

- **LLM reasoning:** reasoning-trajectory analysis and training-free, inference-time steering
- **Natural language processing:** tokenization, language modeling, and efficient model training
- **Vision-language models:** CLIP representations, multimodal evaluation, and multi-object understanding
- **Trustworthy machine learning:** model behavior, reliable evaluation, and experimental validation
- **Representation learning:** deep learning for retrieval and medical imaging
